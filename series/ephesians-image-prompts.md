```
Cinematic photorealistic film still, first-century Roman world, historically grounded and reverent, not fantasy. Warm natural light from oil lamps or a small window, muted earthy palette of ochre, umber, olive and soft gold, gentle film grain, shallow depth of field. Vertical 4:5 portrait composition. Keep the main subject in the upper two-thirds of the frame and leave the lower third darker, simple and uncluttered for text overlay. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```
